# FIREWORKS PROJECT HANDOFF

## Project

- Storefront name: Keystone Current Electric — Electrical Contractor Website
- Industry: Residential and commercial electrical contractor
- Market scaffold: Pittsburgh and South Hills, Pennsylvania
- Repository: `https://github.com/colonnamedia/Electrical-Site-1`
- Preview: `https://electrical-site-1.vercel.app/`
- Status: fictional reusable demo; noindex; no live lead delivery or emergency availability

## Pages and reusable sections

- Home, Services, Electrical Repairs, Panel Upgrades, Lighting & EV Charging, Service Areas, Request Service.
- Reusable hero, verified-trust strip, residential/commercial routes, service cards, safe urgent guidance, coverage module, CTA band, detailed intake, and demo disclosures.
- Original generated hero: professional electrician safely inspecting a closed residential panel; optimized as `assets/electrical-hero.webp` and used in the storefront crop `assets/electrical-storefront-preview.webp`.

## Form and CTA

- Primary CTA: Request Service / Request a Call.
- Fields: first name, last name, email, phone, service type, property type, street or community, ZIP, issue/project description, preferred timing, preferred contact, and optional access/operating notes.
- Local browser validation only. The demo does not send, store, monitor, schedule, upload documents, confirm bookings, dispatch, or take payment.
- Safety guidance tells visitors to move away and call 911 or the responsible utility for immediate danger; it does not instruct hazardous repairs or promise emergency response.

## Integrations

- Current: none. Static Vercel site only; no database, auth, payments, form provider, scheduling, CRM, email, call tracking, maps, or uploads.
- Supported after scope: approved email/form provider, CRM or dispatch routing, scheduling, call tracking, analytics, secure photo intake, or payment for approved products.
- Every provider is client-specific. Require credentials in hosting environment variables, privacy and consent language, spam controls, retention decisions, and complete end-to-end testing before activation.

## Client inputs required

- Legal business name, logo, verified phone/email/address, business hours, license/registration/insurance wording, actual residential and commercial services, service boundaries, emergency or after-hours availability, permits/inspection approach, brands, warranties, financing, reviews with sources and consent, real photos with permission, privacy terms, form destination, and analytics preferences.
