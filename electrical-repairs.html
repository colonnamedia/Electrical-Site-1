Urgent safety

Stop and move away if needed.

For fire, smoke, arcing, shock, downed lines, or immediate danger, leave the area and call 911 or the responsible utility. Do not touch exposed wiring or open a panel. This demo does not provide emergency service.

Useful intake

What a real contractor may ask.

Location

Room, unit, floor, property type, ZIP, and whether the issue is isolated or widespread.

Symptoms

What was seen, heard, smelled, or lost—without asking the customer to test unsafe equipment.

Timing

When it started, whether it is continuing, and the client’s actual service availability after launch.
