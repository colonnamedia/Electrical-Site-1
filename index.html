Two customer routes

Homes and businesses ask different questions.

Residential customers can describe rooms, devices, and timing. Commercial customers can identify facility type, operating constraints, tenant coordination, and the best point of contact.

  • Residential repairs, renovations, panels, lighting, generators, and EV readiness
  • Commercial troubleshooting, tenant improvements, lighting, dedicated circuits, and maintenance planning

Safety-oriented guidance

A useful urgent path without a false promise.

The template separates emergency safety advice from service availability. It never tells a visitor to open a panel, touch wiring, reset repeatedly, or attempt a dangerous repair.

Do not rely on this demo

Immediate danger comes first.

For fire, smoke, arcing, shock, downed lines, or another immediate hazard, move away and contact 911 or the responsible utility. A purchased site displays only the client’s verified contact hours and response options.
